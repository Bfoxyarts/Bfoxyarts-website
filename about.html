About the Artist

Artist Statement

Art is both a reflection and a rediscovery of who we are. Through my work, I explore the beauty found in nature, human connection, and personal growth. Each piece is an expression of what inspires me, challenges me, and brings meaning to my life.

Working primarily in acrylic and watercolor, I enjoy incorporating texture and mixed media elements to create depth, movement, and visual interest. My creative practice also includes sketching and photography, which often serve as inspiration for my paintings.

Artist Bio

Bernadette Fox is a New Jersey-based artist whose work is inspired by nature, personal experiences, and the beauty found in everyday life. After a rewarding career in elementary education, she has returned to her artistic roots with renewed passion and purpose.

Through her artwork, she seeks to celebrate resilience, connection, and the joy of rediscovering one's voice.

Believing that creativity has the power to heal, inspire, and connect us, she creates art that celebrates beauty, resilience, and the ongoing journey of self-discovery.
