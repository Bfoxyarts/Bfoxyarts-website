About the Artist

Artist Statement

We are all a work of art. Art is a reflection of the self—a continuous journey of expression and discovery. My work embodies who I am and what I cherish in the world. I find endless inspiration in nature’s beauty, but I am equally drawn to revealing the inner radiance of the people I love.

I primarily work with acrylics and watercolors, but I also explore sketching, crafting, photography, and card-making. I enjoy experimenting with texture and unconventional materials in my paintings to bring depth and movement to the canvas.

Eviction Day depicts the theme, Between Artist, Artwork, and Audience: Emotional Journeys in Art by addressing how negative comments both internal and external impact the lives of women daily. This work is a call to reclaim mental space, to evict self-doubt and criticism, and to embrace our inherent beauty, inside and out.

Artist Bio

I am a 54-year-old New Jersey-based artist rediscovering my creative passion after a career in elementary education.

Though my early years as an art major were met with discouragement, leading me to step away from my artistic path, I am now embracing the opportunity to create once more. This time, I am guided by my passion and intuition—choosing to listen to my heart rather than my critics.
