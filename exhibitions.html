Memento Mori II

Connexions Gallery

213 Northampton St, Easton, PA, 18042

October 10–November 14, 2026

Opening reception: Saturday, October 10, 2026, 7-10PM

Mommento Mori is Latin for "remember that we must die". It's an artistic symbol or trope acting as a reminder of the inevitability of death. Think skulls, graveyards, certain birtds and animals like crows, ravens, vultures, candles, rotting fruit, wilited flowers... subtle or overt.

Perfectly Imperfect | International art exhibit

Inperfecta

809 NW Flanders St, Portland, OR

November 9–30, 2024

Opening reception: Event Conculded

The Imperfecta Art & Design Gallery is pleased to present Perfectly Imperfect, a group art show that features 27 artists based in the US, UK, Italy, Spain, Chile and Mexico. Artists were challenged to explore notions of imperfection and the beauty of imperfect uniqueness by creating monochromatic artworks in any medium.
